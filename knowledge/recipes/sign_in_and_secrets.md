title: Sign-ins, passwords and payment details
triggers: [login, log in, sign in, signin, password, credentials, otp, 2fa, verification code, card number, redacted]
---
Servo never types passwords, one-time codes or card numbers, even if the user pasted them (they are redacted before you see them).
1. Navigate to the sign-in page if asked (e.g. `go to gmail`).
2. You may `type "<email or username>" into <field>` if the user gave it.
3. Then stop: return no further subtasks and say in "reply" that they should enter the password themselves and tell you when they're signed in.
