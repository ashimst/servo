title: Volume and media playback
triggers: [volume, sound, louder, quieter, mute, unmute, next track, previous track]
---
Volume uses system media keys; each `press volumeup` or `press volumedown` is one step of about 2%,
so "a bit louder" is `press volumeup` written 5 times. `press volumemute` toggles mute.
Play/pause whatever is playing: `press playpause`. Next/previous: `press nexttrack`, `press prevtrack`.
Never open Settings or run commands to change volume.
