id: youtube
title: Play, pause or search on YouTube
triggers: [youtube, video, music video, skip ad, skip the ad]
---
Play something:
1. `go to youtube.com/results?search_query=<words+joined+with+plus>` (opens the
   results page directly; never hunt for the search box)
2. Next round the screen lists result links like "Kanye West - Runaway (Video Version) 4 minutes".
   `click [n]` on the best match (skip "Mix", channel and Shorts links).
3. Done when a "Pause (k)" / "Pause keyboard shortcut k" button is listed, or the window title is the video's name.

Pause / resume: `click [n]` on the "Pause (k)" or "Play (k)" button, or `press k`. Never close the tab to pause.
Ads: if "Skip" or "Skip Ad" is listed, `click [n]` on it; otherwise `wait 5` and look again.
