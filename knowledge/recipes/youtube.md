id: youtube
title: Play, pause or search on YouTube
triggers: [youtube, video, music video, skip ad, skip the ad]
---
Play something:
1. `search youtube for <what to play>` (opens the results page directly; never hunt for the search box)
2. Next round, the screen lists result links like "Kanye West - Runaway (Video Version) 4 minutes, 38 seconds".
   `click <that exact link text>` for the best match (skip "Mix", channel and Shorts links).
3. Done when the screen shows a "Pause (k)" / "Pause keyboard shortcut k" button, or the window title is the video's name.

Pause / resume the current video: `click Pause (k)` or `click Play (k)` (labels vary, e.g. "Pause keyboard shortcut k").
Never use "close" to pause.
Ads: if "Skip" or "Skip Ad" is listed, `click Skip`; otherwise `wait 5` and look again.
